# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in GlitchLens to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Keep discussions about the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and discriminatory jokes or language.
- Public or private harassment, and publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or pull requests.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, Eduardo Maldonado, through [www.emac.dev](https://www.emac.dev), or use GitHub's private reporting tools on this repository. Reports are handled in confidence.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.
